How the map is classified

Two questions are asked of every organisation: which part of the stack it contributes to, and what kind of organisation it is. An organisation can sit on several layers but has one actor type.

Stack layers

Funding

Grants, public budget and private capital that pay for compute, data work and research. What gets funded here decides which capabilities exist at all.

Compute

Data centres, supercomputers and cloud capacity on Swedish soil, together with the hardware partnerships behind them. Covers both training runs and the machines that serve models in production.

Data

Holders of Swedish text, speech, public records and domain corpora, and the work that makes those collections licensable and machine-readable.

Model

Pre-training, fine-tuning and domain adaptation — organisations that build or reshape models rather than only consuming them.

Evaluation

Benchmarks, test sets, red-teaming and measurement of how models behave in Swedish and in Swedish institutional settings.

Deployment

Inference services, APIs, applications and the integration work that puts a model in front of someone who is not its builder.

Adoption

Organisations using AI in their own operations. The demand side: it decides whether the rest of the stack is worth building.

Governance

Law, regulation, standards, procurement rules and public oversight that determine what may be built, and on which data.

Actor types

Gov agency

State agencies and authorities, and the government itself.

Academia/RI

Universities, research institutes and national research infrastructure.

Large corporate

Established companies, Swedish or with substantial Swedish operations.

Startup/SME

Smaller and younger companies, typically founder-led or venture-funded.

Media

Broadcasters and publishers. Kept separate because they hold Swedish text and audio that carries rights.

Funder

Bodies whose role is allocating money rather than doing the work.

NGO/Industry body

Non-profits, academies and member organisations that convene, advise or set norms.
